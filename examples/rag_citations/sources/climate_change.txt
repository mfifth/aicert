Source 1: Climate Change Facts

Global temperatures have risen by 1.1 degrees Celsius since the pre-industrial era. This warming is primarily driven by human activities such as burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial processes. The year 2023 was the warmest year on record, breaking previous temperature records set in 2016 and 2020. Climate scientists agree that limiting warming to 1.5 degrees Celsius is crucial to prevent catastrophic tipping points in the Earth's climate system.

Source 2: Renewable Energy Growth

Solar energy capacity has increased by 22% annually over the past decade, making it the fastest-growing energy source globally. Wind power now accounts for over 8% of global electricity generation. Countries like Denmark, Germany, and China are leading the transition to renewable energy, with Denmark generating over 50% of its electricity from wind power alone. The cost of solar panels has dropped by 89% since 2010, making renewable energy increasingly competitive with fossil fuels.
