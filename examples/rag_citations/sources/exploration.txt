Source 1: Mars Mission Timeline

NASA's Artemis program aims to return humans to the Moon by 2026, establishing a sustainable presence as a stepping stone for future Mars missions. SpaceX's Starship vehicle is designed for Mars colonization, with uncrewed missions planned for the 2020s. The average distance from Earth to Mars is 225 million kilometers, with travel times of approximately 7 months using current propulsion technology. Mars missions face significant challenges including radiation exposure, landing precision, and the need for in-situ resource utilization.

Source 2: Deep Sea Exploration

The Mariana Trench reaches depths of approximately 11,000 meters, making it the deepest point in Earth's oceans. Only 3 expeditions have successfully reached the Challenger Deep, the trench's lowest point, including the 1960 Trieste dive and recent missions in 2019 and 2025. Deep sea environments are characterized by extreme pressure, complete darkness, and temperatures near freezing. Recent discoveries include thousands of new species and unique ecosystems that thrive without sunlight, using chemosynthesis instead of photosynthesis.
